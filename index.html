Biography

Dr. Srikantha N is currently serving in the Department of Mathematics at Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bangalore. He earned his Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) degrees from Kuvempu University. His core areas of research interest span Differential Geometry and Fluid Mechanics.

With over 12 years of teaching experience and 7 years of active research contributions, Dr. Srikantha has published 27 research papers in high-impact peer-reviewed international journals and presented his findings at numerous National and International Conferences. He has successfully guided one Ph.D. scholar to completion and is currently supervising another.

In addition to research, he is an active academic speaker, having delivered multiple invited lectures on mathematical concepts and software tools such as Linear Algebra, GeoGebra, Maple, and Integral Transforms. He is a Life Member of the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) and holds a granted UK Design Patent in thermal testing apparatus.
